I love bringing science into the classroom! My school visits are designed to be interactive, hands-on, and fun, helping students of all ages explore topics like honey and bee products, the ecological relationships between microbes and bees, and fungi in health and disease.

Each visit is tailored to the age group and curriculum, encouraging curiosity and giving students the chance to ask questions, handle specimens safely, and see science in action. If you're interested in a talk or a workshop for your school, feel free to reach out - my contact details can be found on the About page.
